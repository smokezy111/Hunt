Privacy Policy

H.U.N.T · by Smokezyiidk · Last updated: November 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information H.U.N.T (the "Game"), developed by Smokezyiidk ("we", "us", "our"), collects when you play it on Meta Quest, how that information is used and shared, how long it is kept, and how you can ask us to delete it. By playing the Game, you agree to this policy.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Smokezyiidk is responsible for the information described in this policy. You can contact us at any time at smokezyiidk@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We only collect what the Game needs to work, mainly to let you play online with other players.

From your Meta account (via the Meta Platform SDK), with your permission:

  • User ID — an app-scoped ID that Meta gives the Game to identify your account. It is unique to this Game and is not your Meta account email or password.
  • User profile — your Meta username / display name (and profile picture, if the platform provides one).

Information created while you play online:

  • Network information — your IP address and connection details, which are needed to connect you to multiplayer sessions.
  • Gameplay data — your in-game position, movement, avatar/head/hand tracking pose, and in-game actions, which are sent to other players in your session so they can see you.
  • Voice chat — if you use voice chat, your microphone audio is streamed live to other players in your session. We do not record or store voice audio.
  • Text chat — if you send text messages in game, they are shown to other players in your session.

We do NOT collect: your real name, email address, phone number, payment details, precise location, contacts, photos, or camera/passthrough images. We do not use advertising IDs and we do not sell your data.

3. How we use your information

InformationWhy we use it
User IDTo identify your account in multiplayer, keep your progress or settings (if any), and enforce bans or moderation against players who break the rules.

4. How your information is shared

  • Other players — your display name, avatar, movement, voice and chat are visible to the other players in the same multiplayer session. Please do not share personal information in chat.
  • Meta — the Game runs on the Meta Quest platform and uses Meta's services (such as the Meta Platform SDK). Meta's handling of your data is covered by the Meta Privacy Policy.
  • Hosting / networking providers — if multiplayer sessions run on servers or relay services we rent, those providers process network and gameplay data only to deliver the service to us.
  • Legal reasons — we may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the safety of players or the Game.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone.

5. How long we keep your information

  • Gameplay, voice and text chat data is transmitted in real time and is not stored after your session ends.
  • Network information may appear in temporary server logs, which are deleted within 30 days.
  • Your User ID and display name are kept only as long as needed to run the Game (for example, for saved progress or ban records). We delete them when you ask us to, or when they are no longer needed.

6. Your choices and how to delete your data

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the information we hold about you at any time:

  1. Email smokezyiidk@gmail.com with the subject line "H.U.N.T Data Deletion Request".
  2. Include your Meta username so we can find your data.
  3. We will confirm and complete your request within 30 days.

You can also stop all data collection by uninstalling the Game, and you can manage the permissions the Game has to your Meta account in your Meta Quest / Meta Horizon app settings. You can mute or block other players in game, and you can turn off your microphone at any time.

7. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). Players aged 10–12 may only use Meta Quest through a parent-managed Meta account. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us at smokezyiidk@gmail.com and we will delete it.
